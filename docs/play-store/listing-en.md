## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)
7UI is a home screen launcher for people who like their phone tidy and fast to move around. Swipe up for your apps, swipe sideways through your pages, and long-press anything for quick actions.

HOME SCREEN
• As many pages as you like, with a dock and an optional search bar
• Folders with five colors
• Widgets you can move and resize
• Edit mode (pinch or long-press empty space): add and delete pages, and choose your default page
• Optional page looping

APPS SCREEN
• Paged grid instead of an endless list, arranged the way you want
• Choose a 4x5, 5x5 or 5x6 grid
• Live search and quick page jumps with the page indicator

GESTURES THAT FEEL RIGHT
• The Apps screen follows your finger and settles by the speed of your swipe
• Each swipe moves in one direction only, so pages never flip by accident

QUICK OPTIONS
• Long-press an icon for app shortcuts, app info, remove and uninstall
• Add to Home asks which page the app should go on

SIDE PANEL
• A slim handle on the screen edge opens your favorite apps from anywhere
• Left or right side, hidden in landscape

BACKUP AND RESTORE
• Save your layout and settings to a file and bring them back any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet access, no ads, no tracking. Your layout never leaves your phone.

Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Ukrainian and Chinese.

7UI is free for personal and other noncommercial use.

## Permission notes for review
- Display over other apps: draws the optional Side panel handle and panel over other apps, only when the user turns the Side panel on.
- Request delete packages: opens the system uninstall dialog when the user picks Uninstall.
- Expand status bar: opens the notification panel when the user swipes down on the Home screen.
