# 7UI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

7UI is a home screen launcher for Android, developed by an independent developer (Sim).

## Data collection

7UI does not collect, store remotely, share or sell any personal data. It has no internet access, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Data on your device

To work as a launcher, 7UI uses the following on your phone only:

- **Installed apps:** the list of apps, their names, icons and shortcuts, to show them on the Home and Apps screens.
- **Your layout and settings:** pages, dock, folders, widgets and preferences, saved in 7UI's private app storage.
- **Widgets:** shown through Android's standard widget system; their content comes from the apps that provide them.

None of this leaves your device.

## Backups

The "Back up layout and settings" option writes a file to a location you choose, using Android's file picker. 7UI does not upload it anywhere; where it is stored (for example a cloud drive) is your choice.

## Permissions

- **Display over other apps:** used only to show the Side panel handle and panel, and only if you turn the Side panel on.
- **Request to delete packages:** used only to open Android's own uninstall dialog when you choose Uninstall.
- **Expand status bar:** used only to open the notification panel when you swipe down on the Home screen.

## Children

7UI does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
